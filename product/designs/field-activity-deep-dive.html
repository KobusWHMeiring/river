🌿 Weeding / Invasive Removal

Zero weeding metrics have been logged. Ever. This is despite:
• 12 weeding tasks planned across July–August
• The weeding data capture feature being built (species tracking with +/- buttons)
• Weeding being one of the core field activities

Possible causes: (a) Weeding IS happening but the team isn't logging metrics during visits, (b) the weeding UI is confusing or hidden, (c) weeding is being logged as general notes instead of metrics, or (d) weeding tasks are planned but fieldwork hasn't started yet in those sections.

Three Takeaways

① Data is concentrated in 3 sections.
Fynbos Snake, Mowbray, and Observatory account for nearly 100% of all logged field activity. This could reflect genuine operational focus, or it could mean the other sections are doing work that isn't being recorded.
② Weeding is invisible.
0 weeding metrics despite weeding being a core activity type. If weeding is happening, it’s either logged as free-text notes (not metrics) or not logged at all. The Enhanced Weeding Data Capture feature in the backlog may need UI changes to make it more discoverable.
③ Rich ecological data exists but isn't surfaced.
257 plants of 26 indigenous species — this is impressive restoration data. It’s in the database but the dashboard doesn't show species breakdowns. Item #2 (participant count) and the detailed planting metrics (backlog #12) would turn this hidden data into visible impact.
